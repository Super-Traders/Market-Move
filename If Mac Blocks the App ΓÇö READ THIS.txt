====================================================================
 MAC SAYS: "Apple could not verify ... is free of malware"?
 THIS IS NORMAL. Here is the one-time fix (takes 30 seconds).
====================================================================

Apple shows this warning for ANY app downloaded from the internet
that isn't from the App Store. It does NOT mean anything is wrong.
You only have to do this ONCE.

STEP 1.  In the warning popup, click  "Done"
         (do NOT click "Move to Bin")

STEP 2.  Open  System Settings  ->  Privacy & Security

STEP 3.  Scroll down to the "Security" section. You will see:
         '"Start Screener (Mac).command" was blocked...'
         Click the  "Open Anyway"  button next to it.

STEP 4.  Double-click  "Start Screener (Mac).command"  again.
         If asked once more, click  "Open".

That's it. From now on it opens normally with a double-click.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
ALTERNATIVE (if you're comfortable with Terminal):
Open Terminal, type  bash  followed by a space, then drag the
"Start Screener (Mac).command" file into the Terminal window and
press Return. This starts the app directly without the popup.
--------------------------------------------------------------------

On OLDER Macs (macOS 14 or earlier) there's a shortcut instead:
right-click the file -> Open -> Open.
